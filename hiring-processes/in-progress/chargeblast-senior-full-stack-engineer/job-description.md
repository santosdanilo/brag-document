# Chargeblast - Senior Full Stack Engineer

**Source:** [Amplify IT careers listing](https://www.amplifyit.io/careers/chargeblast/full-stack-engineer)
**Location:** Remote to United States of America
**Level:** Senior
**Function:** Full Stack Engineering

## Company

Chargeblast is a fintech company building payment and chargeback management solutions for merchants. Its platform focuses on fraud prevention, revenue recovery, payment strategy optimization, and replacing manual financial workflows with automated, data-driven operations.

## Responsibilities

- Develop and maintain robust web applications for payment and chargeback operations.
- Own features end to end, including scalable APIs, data layers, and user interfaces.
- Build high-performance, data-rich dashboards and operational tools for large datasets, including tables, filters, exports, and charts.
- Identify and automate complex manual financial workflows.

## Requirements

- Production backend engineering with Go and/or Node.js, including APIs, services, and background processing systems.
- At least 3+ years of full-stack development experience, ideally with internal tools or operational dashboards for critical business processes.
- Strong TypeScript skills and hands-on Angular or a similar modern frontend framework.
- Relational database experience, particularly PostgreSQL, including schema design, optimized queries, and data management.
- Rigorous attention to financial correctness and data integrity in payment and chargeback systems.

## Technology

Go, Node.js, PostgreSQL, TypeScript, Angular, Redis, AWS.
