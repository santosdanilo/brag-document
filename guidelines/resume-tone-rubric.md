# Resume Tone Rubric

Use this rubric for resume summaries, experience bullets, skills positioning, and cover letters. The target tone is precise, confident, factual, and restrained.

## Required Checks

Every resume bullet must pass all checks below before PDF generation:

1. **Traceable:** The claim maps to a confirmed evidence-ledger entry or an explicit canonical source reference.
2. **Outcome-led:** Starts with the problem solved, value delivered, or capability enabled when the evidence supports it.
3. **Active:** Uses a clear action verb and avoids passive constructions.
4. **Specific:** Names the relevant technology, system, responsibility, or constraint without unnecessary implementation detail.
5. **Evidence-backed:** Uses a metric, scope, before/after result, or concrete deliverable when documented.
6. **Business-readable:** A non-technical recruiter can understand the problem and outcome.
7. **Concise:** Removes filler and keeps one primary idea per bullet.
8. **Accurate in seniority:** Does not inflate ownership, team scope, architecture authority, or business impact.
9. **Free of internal jargon:** Replaces internal component names, endpoint names, and codebase terms with their business or technical purpose.
10. **Natural:** Sounds like a specific professional account, not generic AI-generated marketing copy.
11. **Standalone:** States the outcome and action clearly enough to be understood without a second explanatory sentence.

## Preferred Language

- Use verbs such as **delivered**, **reduced**, **integrated**, **modernized**, **designed**, **established**, **migrated**, **resolved**, and **documented** when they accurately describe the evidence.
- Prefer concrete qualifiers such as **business-critical**, **real-time**, **production**, **modular**, or **data-rich** only when supported by the source.
- Put metrics after the outcome rather than making the number the subject of the sentence.

## Avoid

- Unsupported superlatives: "world-class", "exceptional", "unparalleled", "amazing".
- Generic claims: "results-driven", "team player", "passionate", "hard-working".
- Inflated ownership: "owned the entire platform", "transformed the organization", or "led strategy" without evidence.
- Vague impact: "improved performance" without what improved or how it was measured.
- Keyword stuffing: listing a job-description technology without documented experience.
- Repeated AI patterns: every bullet beginning with "Spearheaded", excessive em dashes, or identical sentence structures.

## Approval Gate

- **Pass:** All required checks pass and every claim is confirmed.
- **Revise:** The claim is supported but fails clarity, concision, tone, or outcome framing.
- **Hold:** The claim needs confirmation or relies on an unsupported inference.

Do not generate the final PDF while any selected claim is marked **Hold**.
