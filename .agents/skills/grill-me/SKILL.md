Interview me relentlessly about every aspect of this plan until we reach a shared understanding. Walk down each branch of the design tree, resolving dependencies between decisions one-by-one. For each question, provide your recommended answer.

Ask the questions one at a time.

If a question can be answered by exploring the codebase, explore the codebase instead.

## Career Content Mode

When improving professional experience, resumes, cover letters, or interview stories:

- Read the repository's source-of-truth files before asking questions.
- Ask one question at a time and stop after each question for the user's answer.
- Resolve the story in this order: situation, task or responsibility, personal actions, decisions and trade-offs, collaboration, result, evidence, and lessons learned.
- Spend most of the questioning on the user's actions and contribution, not generic team activity.
- Provide a recommended interpretation for every question, based only on confirmed repository evidence.
- Use STAR as the internal structure; do not force the literal labels into resume bullets.
- Rewrite the final bullet toward outcome and contribution: `[impact] + [personal action] + [evidence]`.
- Classify facts as confirmed, needs confirmation, or do not claim. Never invent metrics, ownership, scope, or tool usage.
